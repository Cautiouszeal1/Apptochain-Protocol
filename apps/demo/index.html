apptochain sandbox

Builder Alpha · Phase 2

Connect. Map. Launch.

This sandbox walks through the three steps with real contracts. As the builder you deploy a membership pass and a points program. Then, as a user, you claim a pass and earn points without paying gas.

STEP 01 · CONNECT

Your wallet

Not connected.

STEP 02 · LAUNCH (builder)

Deploy your modules

One transaction each. You become the owner. Addresses are remembered in this browser.

STEP 03 · MAPPED ACTIONS (user, gasless)

Use the app

The SDK maps these two app features to on-chain actions. You only sign a request, and the relayer pays the gas.
