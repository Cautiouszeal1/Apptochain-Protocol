01 · What it is

The shortest road from app to chain.

Most teams want wallets, tokenised ownership and on-chain payments but lack the time to rebuild. apptochain wraps what you already run.

STEP 01

Connect

Add the SDK and link your app. Login, wallets and identity are handled for you.

STEP 02

Map

Choose which actions become on-chain: payments, memberships, assets, rewards.

STEP 03

Launch

Deploy to your preferred network. Your users keep ownership, you keep your product.

02 · Revenue model

How apptochain earns.

Revenue comes from usage of the platform, not from selling hype. Every stream below is tied to a real service delivered to builders.

STREAM A

Deployment fees

A small fee each time an app is mapped and deployed on-chain.

STREAM B

Pro subscriptions

Monthly plans for managed nodes, analytics, higher limits and priority support.

STREAM C

Transaction share

A tiny percentage of payments processed through apptochain rails.

STREAM D

Marketplace

Commission on plugins and templates sold by community developers.

Builders pay→Treasury→Development · Security · Rewards→Community vote

Fee levels and treasury allocation are set through governance and published before launch.
